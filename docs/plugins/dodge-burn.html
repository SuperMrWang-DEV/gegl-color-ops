双曲线磨皮

plugin/dodge-burn 磨皮 Retouch 修饰

提亮一条曲线、压暗一条曲线，各自压在一张全黑蒙版下。涂到哪里，哪里才变亮或变暗——没涂的地方与原图逐像素相同，所以可以反复叠加、随时反悔。

菜单位置
  • SuperMrWang → Retouch → 双曲线磨皮
  • 图层右键 → SuperMrWang → Retouch → 双曲线磨皮

这几条是同一个动作，处理的是当前选中的那一个图层。图层组不行——组没有自己的像素，会直接报错并提示先合并。

脚本调用
lb-plugin-dodge-burn(image, drawables, amount, group)

标准 PDB 过程，参数留空走默认值。

依赖

靠下面这些算子干活。它们已经打进这个插件的下载包（modules-windows-x64 / modules-linux-x64），不用另外去下：

  • gimp:curves

生成的结构

两层 · 黑蒙版
双曲线磨皮              组 · 穿透（带 alpha 时组上还有蒙版）
  ├─ 2 · 压暗（黑蒙版）  正常 · 曲线下压
  ├─ 1 · 提亮（黑蒙版）  正常 · 曲线上提
  └─ 原图层（不动，可以隐藏）

两层都是「正常」模式，各挂一条 gimp:curves（值通道）； 蒙版全黑，所以刚建好时两层根本不参与合成——结果与原图逐像素相同。

涂的是蒙版，不是图层

点中图层的蒙版缩略图（白框套住的那张小图，不是图层缩略图）， 选柔边画笔、白色，不透明度 10–20%。涂到的地方蒙版变白，那一层才显形： 涂提亮层变亮、涂压暗层变暗。涂过头就把前景色换成黑，盖回去。

曲线有多强

12%

「强度」参数是曲线在中间调偏离对角线的幅度：12 表示 0.5 处抬 0.12， 约 30 码。默认 12，范围 1–40。宁少勿多——一层不够就再涂一遍， 比一次涂死好收。

怎么修

  1. 提亮：额头、鼻梁、颧骨高点、眼下三角区——把该亮的地方推上去， 脸就立起来了。
  2. 压暗：脸颊两侧、下颌线、鼻翼阴影、眼窝——把该退的地方收回去。
  3. 每涂一遍就切到下一个图层顺序（提亮 → 压暗 → 再看），避免一路加亮 把皮肤涂成塑料。
  4. 想检查涂了多少：按住 Alt 点蒙版缩略图，只看蒙版；黑白分布 一目了然。

实测

check_retouch.py

恒等往返浮点 0 码、8 位 0 码（蒙版全黑，两层不参与）； 蒙版涂白处严格等于曲线在该点的值；没涂的地方一位不动；带 alpha 的图 透明度交给组蒙版，零误差。

Dodge & Burn

plugin/dodge-burn Retouching Retouch

One curve to lighten, one to darken, each under a fully black mask. Paint where you want it and only there does the image get lighter or darker — everywhere else stays pixel-identical, so you can keep stacking and undo at any time.

Menu location
  • SuperMrWang → Retouch → Dodge & Burn
  • Layer right-click → SuperMrWang → Retouch → Dodge & Burn

These are all the same action, applied to the one layer you have selected. Layer groups do not work — a group has no pixels of its own, so the plug-in stops with an explicit error and asks you to merge first.

Scripting
lb-plugin-dodge-burn(image, drawables, amount, group)

A standard PDB procedure; leave an argument empty to take its default.

Dependencies

The operators below do the work. They are already inside this plug-in's download (modules-windows-x64 / modules-linux-x64) — nothing else to fetch:

  • gimp:curves

What gets created

two layers, black masks
Dodge & Burn group · pass-through (plus a mask when there is alpha)
 ├─ 2 · Darken (black mask)  Normal · curve pulled down
 ├─ 1 · Lighten (black mask) Normal · curve pushed up
 └─ original layer (untouched, can be hidden)

Both layers are in Normal mode with one gimp:curves (value channel) each; the masks are fully black, so the moment the group is created the two layers do not take part in the composite — pixel-identical to the original.

You paint the mask, not the layer

Click the layer's mask thumbnail (the small image with the white frame, not the layer thumbnail), pick a soft brush, white, opacity 10–20%. Where you paint the mask turns white and that layer shows through: painting the lighten layer brightens, painting the darken layer darkens. Overdone? Switch the foreground to black and paint it back.

How strong the curve is

12%

The "strength" parameter is how far the curve deviates from the diagonal at midtones: 12 means 0.12 at 0.5, about 30 codes. Default 12, range 1–40. Err on the weak side — one more pass is easier to take than one too many.

How to work with it

  • Lighten: forehead, bridge of the nose, cheekbone highlights, the triangle under the eyes — push up what should be forward and the face reads as three dimensional.
  • Darken: the sides of the cheeks, jawline, shadow beside the nose, eye sockets — pull back what should recede.
  • Alternate as you go (lighten → darken → look again) instead of adding brightness in one direction until the skin looks plastic.
  • To check how much you painted, Alt-click the mask thumbnail to view the mask on its own — the black and white distribution is obvious.

Measured

check_retouch.py

Identity round trip 0 codes in float and 0 codes in 8-bit (the masks are black, the layers do not take part); where the mask is white the result equals the curve value exactly; untouched pixels do not move by one bit; images with alpha keep transparency through the group mask, zero error.
