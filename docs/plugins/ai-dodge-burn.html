双曲线磨皮（AI 预修）

plugin/ai-dodge-burn 磨皮 Retouch 修饰

还是提亮 / 压暗两条曲线层，区别是蒙版已经不是全黑：插件先把参考 图和原图的亮度差算出来，折成蒙版浓度，预先涂上七成。打开文件就是「已经 打过一轮双曲线」的状态。

菜单位置
  • SuperMrWang → Retouch → 双曲线磨皮（AI 预修）
  • 图层右键 → SuperMrWang → Retouch → 双曲线磨皮（AI 预修）

这几条是同一个动作，处理的是当前选中的那一个图层。图层组不行——组没有自己的像素，会直接报错并提示先合并。

脚本调用
lb-plugin-ai-dodge-burn( image, drawables, prompt, source, amount, prepaint, radius, denoise, server, checkpoint, steps, group)

标准 PDB 过程，参数留空走默认值。

依赖

只用 GIMP 自带的 gimp:curves，不需要额外的算子模块。但要 plugin/_shared/ 里的 retouch_prep.py、retouch_math.py、ai_client.py——下载包里已经带上了。

  • gimp:curves

生成的结构

预涂蒙版
双曲线磨皮（AI 预修）      组 · 穿透
  ├─ 2 · 压暗（已预涂）    正常 + 压暗曲线 + 预涂蒙版
  ├─ 1 · 提亮（已预涂）    正常 + 提亮曲线 + 预涂蒙版
  └─ 原图层（不动）

暗于参考图的地方落在提亮蒙版，亮于参考图的地方落在压暗蒙版；背景与 没差值的地方仍然是黑的（不参与合成）。

浓度不是全图一个系数

曲线是 x + d·4x(1−x)：两端不动、中间最鼓。所以同一个「提亮 0.06」，在中灰需要 蒙版 0.5，在暗部要浓得多。插件按每个像素 自己的亮度反算浓度，而不是全图乘一个数。

为什么只补七成

prepaint 70%

一次补平通常过头——参考图本身也有误差。默认 70%，剩下的交给你的画笔； 想完全交给 AI 就设 100%，只想做个提示就设 30%。设 0% 等于原版插件 （两张全黑蒙版）。

接着怎么修

  1. 点中某一层的蒙版缩略图，柔边画笔、白色、不透明度 10–20%。
  2. 不够亮的位置再补白；画过头用黑色盖回去，或者把预涂浓度调低重跑。
  3. 想检查涂了多少：按住 Alt 点蒙版缩略图，只看黑白分布。

实测

check_retouch_ai*.py

软斑测试图上，暗斑处提亮蒙版 ≈ 116、亮斑处压暗蒙版 ≈ 129， 交叉位置为 0；prepaint=0 时两张蒙版全黑（与原版行为一致）； 选区外的蒙版保持 0。

参数：source、amount 1–40%、 prepaint 0–100%、radius（本地参考）、 denoise、prompt、group。

Dodge & Burn (AI prep)

plugin/ai-dodge-burn Retouching Retouch

Still two curves, one lightening and one darkening — but the masks are no longer black. The plug-in measures how far the reference sits above or below your layer, turns that into mask density and paints 70% of it for you.

Menu location
  • SuperMrWang → Retouch → Dodge & Burn (AI prep)
  • Layer right-click → SuperMrWang → Retouch → Dodge & Burn (AI prep)

These are all the same action, applied to the one layer you have selected. Layer groups do not work — a group has no pixels of its own, so the plug-in stops with an explicit error and asks you to merge first.

Scripting
lb-plugin-ai-dodge-burn( image, drawables, prompt, source, amount, prepaint, radius, denoise, server, checkpoint, steps, group)

A standard PDB procedure; leave an argument empty to take its default.

Dependencies

Only GIMP's own gimp:curves — no extra operator modules. It does need retouch_prep.py, retouch_math.py and ai_client.py from plugin/_shared/, and they are inside the download.

  • gimp:curves

What it builds

pre-painted masks
Dodge & Burn (AI prep)   group · pass-through
  ├─ 2 · Burn (pre-painted)  normal + darken curve + painted mask
  ├─ 1 · Dodge (pre-painted) normal + lighten curve + painted mask
  └─ the original layer (untouched)

Areas darker than the reference land in the dodge mask, brighter ones in the burn mask; flat areas and the background stay black and take no part.

Density is per pixel, not global

The curve is x + d·4x(1−x): pinned at both ends, strongest in the middle. Lifting by 0.06 needs mask 0.5 at mid-grey and a much denser mask in the shadows, so the density is solved from each pixel's own value.

Why only 70%

prepaint

A full correction usually overshoots — the reference has its own error. 70% leaves the last part to your brush; set 100% to hand it all over, 30% for a hint, 0% for the original plug-in (two black masks).

Finishing it

  1. Click a layer's mask thumbnail, pick a soft white brush at 10–20%.
  2. Paint more where it is not there yet; black takes a stroke back, or lower prepaint and run again.
  3. Alt-click the mask thumbnail to look at the mask alone.

Measured

check_retouch_ai*.py

On the soft-blob test image the dark blob lands at ≈ 116 in the dodge mask and the bright one at ≈ 129 in the burn mask, with zero at the crossover; prepaint=0 gives two fully black masks, exactly like the original plug-in, and everything outside a selection stays 0.

Arguments: source, amount 1–40%, prepaint 0–100%, radius (local reference), denoise, prompt, group.
