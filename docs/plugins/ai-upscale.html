AI 超分放大

plugin/ai-upscale AI AI

用放大网络（4x-UltraSharp 一类 ESRGAN）把图层放大 2~4 倍——放大的是细节， 不是插值出来的糊。GIMP 自己的缩放只会变平滑，这块是硬缺口。节点是 ComfyUI 自带的， 只需要一个 64 MB 的权重，不用装任何扩展。

菜单位置
  • SuperMrWang → AI → AI Upscale…

处理对象是当前图层：放大结果是一层新图层，位置按倍率对齐原图层，原图层原样留着。和「图像 → 缩放图像」不是一回事。

脚本调用
lb-plugin-ai-upscale( image, drawables, model, factor, server, timeout)

标准 PDB 过程，参数留空走默认值。

依赖

不依赖任何自研算子，也不用编译：放大走 ComfyUI 自带的 UpscaleModelLoader / ImageUpscaleWithModel，只需要 models/upscale_models/ 里有一个权重（4x-UltraSharp 64 MB）。补权重跑一次 tools/setup_comfy.py --only upscale。

倍率怎么来的

原生 4 倍

ESRGAN 一类都是 4 倍网络：要 2 倍就先 4 倍再 lanczos 缩一半，比直接 2 倍 插值清楚得多，也快（时间只跟原图大小有关）。3 倍同理。

放大只对「本来有信息、只是分辨率不够」的图有用；严重失焦或压缩到发糊的图， 放大会把噪点和块效应一起放大。

先算像素，再动手

4000×3000 放 4 倍是 1.9 亿像素，GIMP 拖不动、内存也扛不住。插件在发请求 之前就把结果尺寸算出来，超了直接说清楚「用更小的倍率，或者先裁出你要的那块」， 而不是跑到一半崩掉。

实测

744×1120 → 1488×2240（2 倍，实际走 4 倍再回缩）：10 秒（RTX 3070 Ti， 4x-UltraSharp）。皮肤会略微「干净」一点，发丝与布料边缘明显变实，没有出现 塑料感或白边。

换模型只要把权重丢进 models/upscale_models/，下拉里就会出现； 没装的话跑一次 tools/setup_comfy.py --only upscale。

AI Upscale

plugin/ai-upscale AI AI

Upscale a layer 2–4× with a real upscaler network (4x-UltraSharp and friends) — new detail, not interpolation. GIMP's own scaling only smooths, which is the gap this fills. The nodes ship with ComfyUI; all you need is a 64 MB weight file.

Menu location
  • SuperMrWang → AI → AI Upscale…

It works on the current layer: the result is a new layer placed at the scaled offset, and the original stays untouched. This is not Image → Scale Image.

Scripting
lb-plugin-ai-upscale( image, drawables, model, factor, server, timeout)

A standard PDB procedure; leave an argument empty to take its default.

Dependencies

The operators below do the work. They are already inside this plug-in's download (modules-windows-x64 / modules-linux-x64) — nothing else to fetch:

Where the factor comes from

native 4×

ESRGAN-class models are 4× networks: asking for 2× means rendering 4× and scaling down with lanczos, which stays sharper than a direct 2× interpolation and costs the same time (time depends on the source size only). 3× works the same way.

It only helps where the information is there and merely under-sampled; a badly defocused or over-compressed image will have its noise and blocking enlarged too.

Pixel budget first

4000×3000 at 4× is 190 megapixels — GIMP cannot hold that layer. The plug-in computes the result size before sending anything and says “use a smaller factor, or crop the part you need” instead of dying halfway through.

Measured

744×1120 → 1488×2240 (2×, rendered at 4× then scaled): 10 s on a 3070 Ti with 4x-UltraSharp. Skin gets slightly cleaner, hair and fabric edges get visibly tighter; no plastic look, no white halos.

Drop another weight into models/upscale_models/ and it appears in the drop-down; if there is none, run tools/setup_comfy.py --only upscale.
