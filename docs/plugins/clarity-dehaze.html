清晰度 / 去朦胧

plugin/clarity-dehaze 影调 Color

Camera Raw「基本」面板上最常拖的两个滑块：清晰度（中频局部 对比，不是锐化）与去朦胧（暗通道先验反解大气散射）。两组参数落在 同一个算子里，先去雾再清晰度，一次算出。

菜单位置
  • SuperMrWang → Color → 清晰度 / 去朦胧…
  • 图层右键 → SuperMrWang → Color → 清晰度 / 去朦胧…

这几条是同一个动作，处理的是当前选中的那一个图层。图层组不行——组没有自己的像素，会直接报错并提示先合并。

脚本调用
lb-plugin-clarity-dehaze(image, drawables, settings, output)

标准 PDB 过程，参数留空走默认值。

依赖

用本项目自己的算子 lb:clarity-dehaze（GIMP 3 没有清晰度 / 去朦胧的等价物；gegl:unsharp-mask 是高频锐化，不是中频对比）。插件包里已附带这个模块，按 readme 放进 GEGL 模块目录即可。

  • lb:clarity-dehaze

清晰度不是锐化

中频，几十像素

锐化（USM）动的是高频：像素级的边缘，效果是「更脆」，噪点也跟着脆。 清晰度动的是中频：几十像素尺度上的明暗过渡——树叶的层次、云里的 体积、皮肤上的大结构。所以它能把一张「平」的照片打开，却不放大噪点。

做法是把亮度减去一个大半径低通，得到中频层 d = Y − low， 再按比例加回去：gain = 1 + 强度 · w · d′ / max(low, 0.08)。

两个细节决定了它「像 PS」而不是「像 USM」： ① 增益是相对量（除以 low），暗部不会因为 low 很小被放大成噪点， 亮部也不会一推就溢出。 ② 中频层先过一道 h·tanh(d/h)：幅度远大于 h 的地方 （硬边缘两侧的过冲）被压到 ±h，边缘不会长出亮边 / 黑边；幅度在 h 以内的 纹理几乎原样通过。这个 h 就是滑杆上的「边缘保护」。

中间调权重

护住黑白两端

增益还乘一个 w = 1 − |2Y − 1|²：中间调满权，越接近纯黑纯白 越不增强。这不是保守，是必要的——暗部本来就没多少可分配的空间，硬推就是 噪点；亮部硬推就是死白。

半径默认 40 px，这是「中频」的边界：再小会滑向锐化（纹理变脆），再大会滑向 全局对比（等于拉曲线）。1920 宽的照片上 40 px 大约相当于画面宽度的 2%。

强度为负时可以当「去清晰度」用：柔化中频但保留细节，比高斯模糊体面得多， 适合人像的皮肤。

去朦胧：雾是一道方程

I = J·t + A(1−t)

远处的山发灰，是空气把大气光 A 按比例混进了景物 J： 透射率 t 越远越小。He、Sun & Tang 2009 的暗通道先验给了 一个便宜的反解：

① 暗通道 dark(p) = min over window(min(R,G,B))； ② 大气光 A 取暗通道最亮的 0.1% 像素的原图均值； ③ t = 1 − ω·dark/lum(A)，夹在 [0.12, 1]； ④ J = (I − A)/t + A。

ω 就是滑杆上的保留雾量，默认 0.95：全解（1.0）看着假， 留一点空气感反而像照片。窗口默认 15 px，要比雾里最亮的物体大， 否则透射率会跟着物体轮廓走，而不是跟着远近走——那种图去完雾像贴了一层灰 补丁。

t 的下限 0.12 是硬约束：再低，1/t 的放大倍数会把噪声乘成十几倍， 暗部直接变彩色斑块。

什么时候不管用

暗通道先验假设「无雾画面里总有一个通道很暗」。天空占满画面、或者 大面积白色主体（雪、白墙）时这个假设不成立，A 会被估到主体上， 去完雾天空发灰、白墙出暗角状色块。这时候把强度降到 30 以下，或者干脆 只用清晰度。

彩色雾（沙尘暴的黄、水雾的青）也不适用：先验假设雾是灰的。PS 的 Dehaze 同样会偏色，只是它有额外一层色温补偿。

负强度等于加雾，偶尔有用：给前景补一点空气透视做纵深。

饱和补偿：去雾会按 1/t 提亮，颜色被拉向大气光的灰白，所以默认 建议给 10–20 的补偿；它只围绕亮度放大色度，色相不动。

两个坑，都在代码里

预览与成品

① 半径要按预览缩放比缩回去。半径是像素单位，预览缩到 560 长边之后， 40 px 的低通相当于原图的 160 px，预览看起来会比成品糊得多，滑杆就没法调。 所以两个半径都乘「预览宽 / 原宽」，并不小于 2。

② 去雾之后必须重算亮度。清晰度用的是亮度 Y，而去雾改过 RGB。 如果沿用去雾前的 Y，低频层和新像素对不上，暗部会出现一圈奇怪的晕。 算子里 dehaze 在前、clarity 在后各自算一遍 Y，就是这个原因。

③ 大气光要看整幅。所以这个算子把整个输入都标成「必需」 （get_required_for_output 返回全图），否则预览和导出会 用两个不同的 A，观感会跳一下。

实测

1600×2400

清晰度 2.03 s、去朦胧 1.21 s、两个都开 3.59 s。 两项都为 0 时走真正的恒等快路径（gegl_buffer_copy）， 0.20 s 里几乎全是图层复制的开销。

清晰度的成本主要是三遍盒式模糊（近似高斯，六个方向 pass）；去朦胧是 单调队列求窗口最小值 + 暗通道直方图，都是 O(N)，窗口再大也不变慢。 预览 560 长边上一次 60–90 ms，拖滑杆跟得上手。

回归 check_clarity_dehaze_gimp.py 24 项： 恒等逐字节、纯色不动、条纹对比升降、硬边相对过冲有界、保护越强过冲越小、 中间调增益大于暗部、合成雾图反解（MAE 40 → 14）、纯雾不爆、负强度加雾、 饱和补偿、极端参数、性能。

脚本怎么调

settings = 一份 JSON

键名与算子属性同名（连字符写法）：

lb-plugin-clarity-dehaze 图层 settings="{\"clarity\": 40, \"dehaze\": 35, \"dehaze-saturation\": 15}" output=replace

全部键：clarity、clarity-radius、 clarity-protect、dehaze、dehaze-radius、 dehaze-keep、dehaze-saturation。 output 三档：filter / replace / new。

没有做的：局部去雾（用深度图或亮度分区只处理天空）、多尺度清晰度 （当前是单一半径）、以及 PS 那种「自动」按钮。前两个都是加参数的问题， 第三个是估参数的问题。

Clarity & Dehaze

plugin/clarity-dehaze Tone Color

The two sliders most people touch first in Camera Raw: Clarity — mid-frequency local contrast, not sharpening — and Dehaze, which inverts atmospheric scattering with the dark channel prior. Both live in one operator: haze first, then clarity, in a single pass.

Menu location
  • SuperMrWang → Color → Clarity & Dehaze…
  • Layer right-click → SuperMrWang → Color → Clarity & Dehaze…

These are all the same action, applied to the one layer you have selected. Layer groups do not work — a group has no pixels of its own, so the plug-in stops with an explicit error and asks you to merge first.

Scripting
lb-plugin-clarity-dehaze(image, drawables, settings, output)

A standard PDB procedure; leave an argument empty to take its default.

Dependencies

Uses this project's own lb:clarity-dehaze operator (GIMP 3 ships nothing equivalent — gegl:unsharp-mask is high-frequency sharpening, not mid-frequency contrast). The module is inside the plug-in zip — drop it into the GEGL module folder.

  • lb:clarity-dehaze

Clarity is not sharpening

mid frequency

Unsharp mask works on high frequency — pixel-scale edges — so the picture gets crisper and the noise gets crisper with it. Clarity works on mid frequency: the tens-of-pixels scale where foliage layers, cloud volume and skin structure live. It opens up a flat photograph without amplifying grain.

Mechanically: subtract a large-radius low pass from luma to get a mid band d = Y − low, then add it back as a ratio: gain = 1 + amount · w · d′ / max(low, 0.08).

Two details are what make it feel like Photoshop rather than USM. ① The gain is relative (divided by low), so shadows are not blown up into noise just because their local average is small, and highlights do not clip the moment you push the slider. ② The mid band goes through h·tanh(d/h) first: wherever the amplitude is far above h — the overshoot on either side of a hard edge — it is squeezed to ±h, so edges grow no bright or dark halo, while texture within h passes through almost untouched. That h is the edge protection slider.

Midtone weighting

both ends protected

The gain is also multiplied by w = 1 − |2Y − 1|²: full weight in the midtones, tapering to nothing at pure black and pure white. That is not caution, it is necessary — shadows have no room to give, so pushing them is just noise, and highlights just go dead white.

The default radius of 40 px is where "mid" ends: smaller slides toward sharpening (texture turns brittle), larger slides toward global contrast (that is a curve, not clarity). On a 1920-wide photo, 40 px is about 2% of the frame width.

Negative amounts are a useful "de-clarity": mid frequencies soften while detail survives — far more presentable than a Gaussian blur, and the usual choice for skin.

Dehaze: fog is an equation

I = J·t + A(1−t)

Distant hills look grey because air mixes atmospheric light A into the scene J in proportion to distance, with transmission t falling off further away. He, Sun & Tang (2009) give a cheap inversion via the dark channel prior:

① dark channel dark(p) = min over window(min(R,G,B)); ② airlight A is the mean of the original pixels at the brightest 0.1% of the dark channel; ③ t = 1 − ω·dark/lum(A), clamped to [0.12, 1]; ④ J = (I − A)/t + A.

ω is the haze kept slider, 0.95 by default: a full solve (1.0) looks fake, and a little atmosphere left in reads as a photograph. The window defaults to 15 px and must be larger than the brightest object inside the haze, otherwise the transmission map follows object outlines instead of depth — those results look like grey patches pasted over the picture.

The 0.12 floor on t is a hard constraint: below it the 1/t amplification multiplies noise tenfold and the shadows turn into coloured blotches.

When it does not work

The prior assumes every hazy-free scene has one very dark channel. A frame filled with sky, or a large white subject (snow, a white wall), breaks that assumption: A lands on the subject itself and the result is a grey sky and vignette-shaped blotches on the white wall. Keep the amount under 30, or use clarity alone.

Coloured haze — yellow dust, cyan spray — does not fit either, since the prior assumes grey fog. Photoshop's Dehaze drifts the same way; it just compensates with an extra temperature pass.

Negative amounts add haze, which is occasionally the point: a bit of aerial perspective gives the foreground depth.

Dehaze saturation: dehazing multiplies by 1/t, pulling colour toward the grey airlight, so 10–20 of compensation usually looks right. It scales chroma around luma, leaving hue alone.

Three traps, all in the code

preview vs result

① Radii must be scaled by the preview ratio. A radius is in pixels; once the preview is reduced to a 560 px edge, a 40 px low pass is worth 160 px on the original and the preview looks far softer than the result — the slider becomes impossible to judge. Both radii are multiplied by preview width / original width and floored at 2.

② Luma has to be recomputed after dehazing. Clarity works on luma, and dehazing has already changed RGB. Reusing the pre-dehaze luma puts the low pass out of step with the new pixels and leaves a halo in the shadows. The operator does dehaze first, then rebuilds Y for clarity — that is why.

③ The airlight needs the whole frame. So this operator marks its entire input as required (get_required_for_output returns the full extent); otherwise the preview and the export would use two different values of A and the look would jump.

Measured

1600×2400

Clarity 2.03 s, dehaze 1.21 s, both 3.59 s. With both amounts at zero it takes a true identity path (gegl_buffer_copy) — the 0.20 s there is almost entirely the layer copy.

Clarity costs three box passes (a Gaussian approximation, six directional passes); dehaze is a monotone deque for the window minimum plus a histogram for the airlight, both O(N), so a larger window costs nothing. A preview at a 560 px edge takes 60–90 ms per update, which keeps dragging responsive.

Regression check_clarity_dehaze_gimp.py, 24 checks: byte-for-byte identity, flat colour untouched, wave contrast up and down, bounded relative overshoot at a hard edge, stronger protection giving less overshoot, midtone gain above shadow gain, synthetic haze inversion (MAE 40 → 14), uniform fog not exploding, negative amount adding haze, saturation compensation, extreme parameters, timing.

Scripting

settings is one JSON string

Keys are the operator's own property names, hyphenated:

lb-plugin-clarity-dehaze 图层 settings="{\\"clarity\\": 40, \\"dehaze\\": 35, \\"dehaze-saturation\\": 15}" output=replace

Full set: clarity, clarity-radius, clarity-protect, dehaze, dehaze-radius, dehaze-keep, dehaze-saturation. output takes filter / replace / new.

Not implemented: local dehazing (depth- or luma-masked, sky only), multi-scale clarity (the radius is single), and a Photoshop-style "Auto". The first two are a matter of more parameters; the third is a matter of estimating them.
