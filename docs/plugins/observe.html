观察层

plugin/observe 观察 Retouch 修饰

修图最怕看不清问题就开始调。这六支把「先建一个观察层」压成一次点击：副本、滤镜、参数都已经配好，看完隐藏或删掉，原图层一个像素都没动。

菜单位置
  • SuperMrWang → Observe → 六个菜单项，点了直接建
  • 图层右键 → SuperMrWang → Observe → 同上

这几条是同一个动作，处理的是当前选中的那一个图层。图层组不行——组没有自己的像素，会直接报错并提示先合并。

脚本调用
lb-plugin-observe-* 六个过程，全部零参数

标准 PDB 过程，参数留空走默认值。

依赖

靠下面这些算子干活。它们已经打进这个插件的下载包（modules-windows-x64 / modules-linux-x64），不用另外去下：

生成的结构

一层副本
观察 · 陡峭曲线        副本 + 图层滤镜（参数随时可改）
原图层                 不动，像素一个都没变

副本是完整的像素副本，滤镜挂在副本上——右键副本 → 图层 → 滤镜就能 把参数调回来，不存在「效果烤死在像素里」这回事。看完删掉整层即可， 不留痕迹。

为什么不做成调整图层

GIMP 3 没有 Photoshop 那种独立调整图层，但图层滤镜是等价的：参数 是活的、像素是死的。差别只在于它必须挂在某个图层上，所以先复制一层 再挂，功能上没有任何损失。

六种看什么

零参数，点了就建
菜单项挂的滤镜看什么
黑白gimp:desaturate（明度） 明暗结构、脏点、凹凸、色块之间的明度差。皮肤问题一大半在这一层 就能看见，是和中性灰、双曲线配合最多的一层
陡峭曲线曲线 S 形，中间调斜率约 2.3 倍 细微色斑、肤色不匀。原本要盯半天才看得出的过渡，被拉成明显的斑
波浪曲线去色 + 锯齿曲线（四个峰谷） 色带、断层、渐变里的台阶。连续的色阶被反复翻折，哪里断了一眼 就露出来——像等高线地形图
饱和度gimp:hue-saturation，+100 泛红、色偏、色块边界。皮肤该均匀的地方一泛红就跳出来
阈值去色 + 硬阶梯（切点＝中位数） 灰尘、痘印、小污点。六种里最狠的一层，只剩形状
海报化gimp:posterize，4 级 大块边界、渐变是否干净。比阈值温和，还留一点结构

阈值为什么按中位数切

写死 0.5 是不行的：偏暗的图（夜景、深色截图、欠曝照片）整张都在 0.5 以下，切出来是一块纯黑，什么都看不见。按图层亮度的中位数切， 黑白天然各占一半。

这里也没用 gimp:threshold：它的 low / high 语义和界面上那两个 滑块并不一致——真实测下来，low == high（想切得干脆）会整片 变黑，区间收窄切点还会飘。改成曲线做硬阶梯，切点就是中位数，行为确定。

两个容易被忽略的细节

波浪和阈值先转灰。直接对彩色图操作，等值线会带上各通道自己的 色相（紫绿蓝的环），信息量大但看着闹，判断「这块肤色不匀」反而更难； 三通道各自过切点还会让阈值图变成彩色。

黑白用明度（Luminance），不用亮度（Lightness）。前者按人眼对 R/G/B 的敏感度加权，红绿两块在 Lightness 下亮度相同、在 Luminance 下 能分开——挑色斑要的正是后者。

Inspection Layers

plugin/observe Inspect Retouch

The most expensive mistake in retouching is adjusting what you cannot see yet. These six turn "build an inspection layer first" into one click: the copy, the filter and its settings are already in place. Hide it or delete it when you are done — not one pixel of the original was touched.

Menu location
  • SuperMrWang → Observe → 六个菜单项，点了直接建
  • Layer right-click → SuperMrWang → Observe → 同上

These are all the same action, applied to the one layer you have selected. Layer groups do not work — a group has no pixels of its own, so the plug-in stops with an explicit error and asks you to merge first.

Scripting
lb-plugin-observe-* 六个过程，全部零参数

A standard PDB procedure; leave an argument empty to take its default.

Dependencies

The operators below do the work. They are already inside this plug-in's download (modules-windows-x64 / modules-linux-x64) — nothing else to fetch:

What gets created

one copy
Observe · Steep curves    a copy + a layer filter (settings stay editable)
original layer            untouched, not one pixel changed

The copy carries the full pixels and the filter sits on the copy: right-click it → Layer → Filters and the parameters are still live. Nothing is baked into pixels, and deleting the layer leaves no trace.

Why not an adjustment layer

GIMP 3 has no stand-alone adjustment layer, but a layer filter is the equivalent: the settings are live, the pixels are not. The only difference is that it has to hang off a layer, so we duplicate one first — no functionality is lost.

What the six show you

no arguments, one click
Menu itemFilterWhat it reveals
Black & Whitegimp:desaturate (luminance) Structure, dust, bumps, brightness differences between patches. Most skin problems are already visible here; this is the layer that pairs with neutral grey and dodge & burn
Steep Curvesan S-curve, midtone slope about 2.3x Faint blotches and uneven tone. Transitions you would stare at for a minute turn into obvious patches
Wave Curvesdesaturate + a four-peak zigzag curve Banding, broken tone steps, steps inside a gradient. Continuous tone folds back and forth, so a break shows up at once — a contour map of the skin
Saturation +100gimp:hue-saturation, +100 Redness, colour casts, the edges of colour patches
Thresholddesaturate + a hard step at the median Dust, acne, small specks. The harshest of the six — only shapes survive
Posterizegimp:posterize, 4 levels Patch boundaries and whether a gradient is clean. Gentler, keeps some structure

Why the threshold uses the median

A fixed 0.5 does not work: a dark image (night shot, dark screenshot, underexposed photo) sits entirely below 0.5 and comes out as one black block with nothing to see. Cutting at the layer's median keeps black and white roughly balanced.

It also avoids gimp:threshold: its low / high arguments do not behave the way the two sliders in the UI do — measured, low == high (the obvious way to get a hard cut) turns the whole layer black, and narrowing the range moves the cut point around. A curve with a one-code step is deterministic: the cut is the median.

Two details that are easy to miss

Wave and threshold desaturate first. Applied to colour, the contours pick up each channel's own hue (purple, green, blue rings) — informative but noisy, and it makes "is this patch uneven?" harder to judge. Letting the three channels cross the threshold separately also turns the threshold layer colour.

Black & white uses luminance, not lightness. Luminance weights R/G/B the way the eye does: a red and a green patch can have the same lightness and still differ in luminance. Spotting blotches needs the latter.
