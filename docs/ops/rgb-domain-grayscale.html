Black & White

lb:rgb-domain-grayscale 影调 逐像素

经典图像编辑器 黑白：六个色相滑块（红/黄/绿/青/蓝/品红）加权转灰，可着色

算法

六个色相滑杆（红 / 黄 / 绿 / 青 / 蓝 / 品红）决定每种颜色对灰度的贡献，结果还可以着色——就是 经典图像编辑器的「黑白」对话框：把蓝拉低压暗天空，把黄推高提亮肤色，其余部分不受影响。

逐像素把三通道排序成 $v_{\max} \ge v_{\text{mid}} \ge v_{\min}$，用到两个权重：最大值通道的色相滑杆，以及夹在中间的那根滑杆（红+绿用黄、绿+蓝用青、蓝+红用品红）：

$$g = (v_{\max}-v_{\text{mid}})\,w_{\max} + (v_{\text{mid}}-v_{\min})\,w_{\text{夹色}} + v_{\min}$$

滑块量程 −200…300 与经典图像编辑器界面一致，默认值（红 40、黄 60、绿 40、青 60、蓝 20、品红 80）取自 经典图像编辑器。

着色

着色走的是经典图像编辑器自己的算法（18 组色相 × 饱和度黑盒标定，逐码吻合，见 bw-cal/RESULTS.md），不是「灰度 × 颜色」的乘法。记着色 $t=(t_R,t_G,t_B)$， NTSC 亮度 $L = 0.3\,t_R + 0.59\,t_G + 0.11\,t_B$（编码域），每个输出通道：

$$\text{out}_{ch} = \begin{cases} (t_{ch}-t_{\min})\cdot \dfrac{g}{\,L-t_{\min}\,}, & g \le L-t_{\min} \quad \text{（底部）} \\[8pt] g + t_{ch} - L, & \text{其余} \quad \text{（中段）} \\[8pt] 1-(t_{\max}-t_{ch})\cdot \dfrac{1-g}{\,t_{\max}-L\,}, & g \ge 1-(t_{\max}-L) \quad \text{（顶部）} \end{cases}$$

再按强度滑杆往灰度回插：$\text{out}_{ch} \leftarrow g + (\text{out}_{ch}-g)\cdot s$。三段在断点处连续，顶/底两段精确过白/黑端点，所以着色后整幅图的明暗层次不会塌掉。

flowchart TD
  A["像素 r, g, b<br/>排序成 max / mid / min"] --> B["按 max–mid 的通道组合<br/>取「最大值色相」+「夹色」两根滑杆"]
  B --> C["g = (max−mid)·w₁ + (mid−min)·w₂ + min"]
  C --> D{"着色开？"}
  D -- "否" --> E["输出中性灰"]
  D -- "是" --> F["三段 SetLum<br/>中段 g + t − L；顶/底段向白 / 黑收缩"]
  F --> G["按强度回插<br/>彩色输出"]

说明

  • 逐像素在 8 位 sRGB 伽马域计算，滑块手感与经典图像编辑器对话框一致。
  • alpha 保留，透明像素仍然透明。
  • 与 GIMP 的「去色」不同：这里的灰度不是固定亮度公式，每种颜色变成什么由你决定， 这正是「黑白」的用处。

Black & White

lb:rgb-domain-grayscale Tone per-pixel

The classic editor's Black & White — six hue sliders decide how much each colour contributes to grey, with an optional tint.

lb:rgb-domain-grayscale · menu Colors → SuperMrWang → Tone → Black & White · GeglOperationPointFilter (per-pixel)

What it does

Six hue sliders — red, yellow, green, cyan, blue, magenta — decide how strongly each colour in the image contributes to the grey result, and the grey can then be tinted. This is the classic editor's Black & White dialog: pull blue down and a blue sky goes dark, push yellow up and skin brightens, without any of it touching the rest of the frame.

The three RGB channels are combined with the six hue weights summed; the slider range −200…300 matches the the classic editor dialog, and the defaults (red 40, yellow 60, green 40, cyan 60, blue 20, magenta 80) are the classic editor's.

How the tint works

The tint is calibrated black-box against the classic editor (18 tints, per-code agreement — see bw-cal/RESULTS.md). It is not a grey × colour multiply. With tint $t=(t_R,t_G,t_B)$ and NTSC luma $L = 0.3\,t_R + 0.59\,t_G + 0.11\,t_B$ (encoding domain), each output channel is

$$\text{out}_{ch} = \begin{cases} (t_{ch}-t_{\min})\cdot \dfrac{g}{\,L-t_{\min}\,}, & g \le L-t_{\min} \quad \text{(bottom)} \\[8pt] g + t_{ch} - L, & \text{otherwise} \quad \text{(middle)} \\[8pt] 1-(t_{\max}-t_{ch})\cdot \dfrac{1-g}{\,t_{\max}-L\,}, & g \ge 1-(t_{\max}-L) \quad \text{(top)} \end{cases}$$

and then pulled back toward grey by strength, $\text{out}_{ch} \leftarrow g + (\text{out}_{ch}-g)\cdot s$. The pieces join continuously and the outer two pass exactly through white and black, so tinting never crushes the tonal range — highlights stay highlights.

flowchart TD
  A["pixel r, g, b<br/>sorted to max / mid / min"] --> B["pick two sliders by the max–mid pair"]
  B --> C["g = (max−mid)·w₁ + (mid−min)·w₂ + min"]
  C --> D{"tint on?"}
  D -- "no" --> E["neutral grey out"]
  D -- "yes" --> F["three-piece SetLum<br/>middle g + t − L, top / bottom collapse"]
  F --> G["blend by strength<br/>colour out"]

Notes

  • The operator runs per pixel in the 8-bit sRGB gamma domain, so the weights behave like the ones in the classic editor's dialog.
  • Alpha is preserved; transparent pixels stay transparent.
  • Unlike GIMP's Colors → Desaturate, the conversion is not a fixed luma formula: you choose what each colour becomes, which is the whole point of Black & White.

Windows module rgb-domain-grayscale.dll, drop it into %LOCALAPPDATA%\gegl-0.4\plug-ins; Linux module rgb-domain-grayscale.so, drop it into ~/.local/share/gegl-0.4/plug-ins, then restart GIMP.
