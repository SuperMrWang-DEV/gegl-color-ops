HSI Levels

lb:hsi-levels 色阶家族 整块区域

HSI 三个分量各自设输入 / 输出黑白场与伽马

菜单：SuperMrWang → Levels → HSI Levels（Filter 类）

概述

对 HSI 的 H / S / I 做独立色阶。HSI 是图像分析里常用的模型：I 是三通道均值，比 HSV 的 max 更接近「这一块有多亮」，S 用 1 − min/I 度量「掺了多少白」，色相则用余弦定义（与 HSV 的六边形角不是同一个角）。

这不是 GIMP/babl 自带的模型，转换在本工程的 common/color-levels.h 里。

色彩空间

分量量程定义
H0–360°cos H = [(R−G)+(R−B)] / (2·√((R−G)²+(R−B)(G−B)))，B>G 时取 360−H
S0–100%S = 1 − min(R,G,B)/I，只掺白不掺黑（掺黑由 I 体现）
I0–100%I = (R+G+B)/3

三点必须说清，否则数值对不上：

  1. H 是余弦定义的角，不是 HSV 的分量比角。两者在纯色上一致（红 0°、 绿 120°、蓝 240°），但在中间色上会差几度。早期版本误用 HSV 的角配 HSI 的 逆变换，实测纯色附近往返差 4 码；现在与逆变换严格互逆，恒等回到 0 码。
  2. S 的分母是 I，不是 max。同一块暗红 HSI 的 S 会比 HSV 的 S 低得多。
  3. I 溢出会裁切：I 接近 1 且 S 很大时，逆变换算出的通道值会超过 1 （HSI 色域不是立方体），按 sRGB 裁到 [0,1]。

逆变换用 HSI 的余弦式，每 120° 一段：

H∈[0,120):  B = I(1−S),  R = I[1 + S·cos H / cos(60−H)],  G = 3I − R − B

（其余两段按 R→G→B 轮换。）

$$z = (h-\text{ia}) \bmod 360,\qquad S_{\text{in}} = (\text{ib}-\text{ia}) \bmod 360,\qquad S_{\text{out}} = (\text{ob}-\text{oa}) \bmod 360$$
$$h' = \bigl(\text{base} + \text{span}\cdot t^{1/\gamma}\bigr) \bmod 360,\qquad (t,\ \text{base},\ \text{span}) = \begin{cases} \left(\dfrac{z}{S_{\text{in}}},\ \text{oa},\ S_{\text{out}}\right), & z \le S_{\text{in}} \\[10pt] \left(\dfrac{z-S_{\text{in}}}{360-S_{\text{in}}},\ \text{oa}+S_{\text{out}},\ 360-S_{\text{out}}\right), & z > S_{\text{in}} \end{cases}$$

$S_{\text{in}}$ 或 $S_{\text{out}}$ 为 0（黑白场重合）时按整圈 $360°$ 处理。

$$u = \operatorname{clamp}\!\left(\frac{x-x_{\text{lo}}}{x_{\text{hi}}-x_{\text{lo}}},\,0,\,1\right), \qquad \text{out} = o_{\text{lo}} + u^{1/\gamma}\,\bigl(o_{\text{hi}}-o_{\text{lo}}\bigr)$$

$o_{\text{lo}} > o_{\text{hi}}$ 时输出反相；结果夹在 $[\min(o_{\text{lo}},o_{\text{hi}}),\ \max(o_{\text{lo}},o_{\text{hi}})]$ 内。

规则细则

  • H 环形重映射：主弧（黑场 → 白场）映到输出弧，剩余弧按比例跟进， 两段共用 gamma；默认参数逐位还原；黑白场重合 = 纯色相旋转。
  • S / I 线性：clamp → 归一化 → gamma → 输出范围，输出反向即反相。 S 归零 → 全图中性灰；I 归零 → 全黑。
  • 黑白场交叉自动交换；alpha ≤ 0 透传。

验证

python tools/check_color_levels.py hsi（3 项）：

用例判据实测
恒等（默认参数）与源图逐像素 ≤1 码最大 0
饱和度归零全图中性（|R−G|、|G−B| ≤2）0
公式对拍Python 复算 RGB→HSI→色阶→HSI→RGB，逐像素 ≤2 码最大 1

公式对拍的参数：H 整体 +25°（整圈旋转）、S 0/100 → 20/80 γ=0.9、I 0/100 → 5/95，覆盖环绕映射、余弦逆变换与量程缩放。

已知事项

  • HSI 与 HSV/HSL 的 S 不可互换：把 HSL 调好的「S=40%」搬到 HSI 上不是同一块 颜色。要跨模型对照，看取色器里的 RGB 值。
  • 高饱和高亮区域往返会有裁切（见上），恒等 0 码是 8 位量化意义下的；浮点 精度下这些像素本身就是越出色域的。
  • 计算量比另外四个大：每像素两次 acosf/sqrtf 级别的运算（另有 3 个分量 查表），4K 图上比 lab-levels 慢一些。

只要这一个

HSI Levels

lb:hsi-levels Levels area

Levels on H, S and I — the image-analysis model whose intensity is the mean of the three channels, not their maximum.

Menu: Colors → SuperMrWang → Tone → HSI Levels (a filter op)

Overview

Independent levels for H, S and I. HSI is the model image analysis likes: I is the mean of the three channels, closer to "how bright is this patch" than HSV's max; S measures "how much white is mixed in" as 1 − min/I; and hue is defined by a cosine, which is a different angle from HSV's hexagon.

This model is not in GIMP/babl; the conversion lives in common/color-levels.h.

Colour space

ComponentRangeDefinition
H0–360°cos H = [(R−G)+(R−B)] / (2·√((R−G)²+(R−B)(G−B))), with 360−H when B>G
S0–100%S = 1 − min(R,G,B)/I — white mixed in only; black is carried by I
I0–100%I = (R+G+B)/3

Three things must be stated or the numbers will not line up:

  1. H is the cosine angle, not HSV's component-ratio angle. They agree on pure colours (red 0°, green 120°, blue 240°) but differ by a few degrees on intermediate ones. An early version mixed HSV's angle with HSI's inverse transform and was off by 4 code values near pure colours; it is now exactly inverse, and identity comes back at 0 codes.
  2. S divides by I, not by max. The same dark red has a much lower S in HSI than in HSV.
  3. I can overflow: with I near 1 and a large S, the inverse transform produces channels above 1 (the HSI gamut is not a cube), which are clipped to [0,1] as sRGB.

The inverse uses HSI's cosine form, one segment per 120°:

H∈[0,120):  B = I(1−S),  R = I[1 + S·cos H / cos(60−H)],  G = 3I − R − B

(The other two segments rotate R→G→B.)

$$z = (h-\text{ia}) \bmod 360,\qquad S_{\text{in}} = (\text{ib}-\text{ia}) \bmod 360,\qquad S_{\text{out}} = (\text{ob}-\text{oa}) \bmod 360$$
$$h' = \bigl(\text{base} + \text{span}\cdot t^{1/\gamma}\bigr) \bmod 360,\qquad (t,\ \text{base},\ \text{span}) = \begin{cases} \left(\dfrac{z}{S_{\text{in}}},\ \text{oa},\ S_{\text{out}}\right), & z \le S_{\text{in}} \\[10pt] \left(\dfrac{z-S_{\text{in}}}{360-S_{\text{in}}},\ \text{oa}+S_{\text{out}},\ 360-S_{\text{out}}\right), & z > S_{\text{in}} \end{cases}$$

A zero $S_{\text{in}}$ or $S_{\text{out}}$ (coincident black / white points) is treated as the full $360°$ circle.

$$u = \operatorname{clamp}\!\left(\frac{x-x_{\text{lo}}}{x_{\text{hi}}-x_{\text{lo}}},\,0,\,1\right), \qquad \text{out} = o_{\text{lo}} + u^{1/\gamma}\,\bigl(o_{\text{hi}}-o_{\text{lo}}\bigr)$$

An inverted output ($o_{\text{lo}} > o_{\text{hi}}$) gives a negative-style inverse; the result is clamped to $[\min(o_{\text{lo}},o_{\text{hi}}),\ \max(o_{\text{lo}},o_{\text{hi}})]$.

Rules

  • Circular hue remap: main arc (black point → white point) onto the output arc, the rest proportionally, both sharing the gamma; bit-exact at defaults; black and white point at one angle is a pure hue rotation.
  • S and I are linear: clamp → normalise → gamma → output range; inverted output is an inverse. S to zero gives neutral grey; I to zero gives black.
  • Crossed black and white points swap automatically; alpha ≤ 0 passes through.

Verification

python tools/check_color_levels.py hsi (3 cases):

CaseCriterionMeasured
identity (defaults)≤1 code per pixel against the sourcemax 0
saturation to zeroneutral everywhere (|R−G|, |G−B| ≤ 2)0

Replay parameters: H +25° overall (a full rotation), S 0/100 → 20/80 with γ=0.9, I 0/100 → 5/95, covering the wrap-around map, the cosine inverse and the range scaling.

Known issues

  • HSI's S is not interchangeable with HSV's or HSL's: an "S=40%" tuned in HSL is not the same colour in HSI. To compare across models, look at the RGB values.
  • Saturated bright regions clip on the round trip (see above); the 0-code identity holds at 8-bit quantisation, and at float precision those pixels are simply out of gamut.
  • It costs more than the other four: per pixel there is acosf/sqrtf-level work (plus three component lookups), so a 4K image is somewhat slower than lab-levels.
