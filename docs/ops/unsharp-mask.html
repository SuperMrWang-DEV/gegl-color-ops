Unsharp Mask / Smart Sharpen

lb:unsharp-mask 锐化 AreaFilter

Photoshop「锐化 → USM 锐化 / 智能锐化」的等价物：用模糊版做参考，把原图与模糊版的差值按百分比加回去。

lb:unsharp-mask · 菜单 SuperMrWang → Sharpen → Unsharp Mask · 单输入

用「原图 − 模糊图」的差值把边缘加陡，是 Photoshop「锐化 → USM 锐化」的等价物，并且把「智能锐化」真正想要的那件事——不让硬边长出白边——做成了一个滑杆。

参数 / Parameters

参数范围默认含义
radius 半径0.1–1002.0参考模糊的半径（σ）。小半径锐化纹理，大半径做局部对比（清澈感）
amount 数量0–500%100差值加回去的比例，PS 的 Amount
threshold 阈值0–2550差值小于这个码数判为平坦区，原样保留，避免放大颗粒
fringe 光晕抑制0–100%100把结果夹到原图 3×3 邻域的 [min, max]，100 时完全不产生过冲

fringe 是这个名字的由来：普通 USM 在硬边两侧各留一条白边/黑边（fringe），夹到邻域极值之后就没有了。

算法

  1. 参考模糊。σ ≤ 4 时用真高斯核做可分离卷积（半宽 3σ）；更大时改用 三次盒式模糊串联——三次盒式的方差是 3·((2r+1)²−1)/12，反解 r = (√(4σ²+1) − 1)/2。代价与半径无关（O(n) 而不是 O(n·σ)）， 而 σ ≥ 4 时两者分歧已小于 1/255。
  2. 差值 d = v − blur。|d| ≤ threshold/255 直接输出原值。
  3. 锐化 v + amount·d。
  4. 光晕抑制：取原图 3×3 的 lo/hi，把上一步结果夹进 [lo, hi]， 再按 fringe 与未夹版本混合。

第 4 步的性质值得说清楚：硬边上它等于不锐化（3×3 里没有过渡可陡化），软边上它把过渡压陡但不过冲。所以 100% 时这张图永远不会多出白边，代价是高光的微弱溢出会被压掉——想要 PS 那种「亮部发光」的锐利感就把 fringe 调到 30–50。

用法 / Recipes

  • 纹理锐化：radius=1.0 amount=120 threshold=3 fringe=100
  • 局部对比（清澈感）：radius=15 amount=40 threshold=0 fringe=60
  • 输出前最后一刀：radius=0.8 amount=90 threshold=2 fringe=100，配合缩图后使用

与 Photoshop 的差异

PS USMPS 智能锐化lb:unsharp-mask

PS 的「锐化 / 进一步锐化 / 锐化边缘」是三个固定参数的 USM 预设，用本算子配 amount 即可复现，不再单独出三个算子。

测试 / Testing

Unsharp Mask

lb:unsharp-mask Sharpen AreaFilter

Sharpen against a blurred copy with the amount, radius and threshold of Photoshop's Unsharp Mask, plus a halo stage that keeps hard edges from growing white fringes.

lb:unsharp-mask · menu SuperMrWang → Sharpen → Unsharp Mask · one input

Steepens edges by adding back the difference between the image and a blurred copy — Photoshop's Unsharp Mask, plus the one thing Smart Sharpen is really after, as a slider.

Parameters

ParameterRangeDefaultMeaning
radius0.1–1002.0σ of the reference blur. Small sharpens texture, large builds local contrast (the clarity look)
amount0–500%100How much of the difference is added back
threshold0–2550Differences below this many levels count as flat area and are left alone
fringe0–100%100Clamp the result to the 3×3 neighbourhood of the original; at 100 there is no overshoot at all

fringe is where the name comes from: plain unsharp masking leaves a bright and a dark fringe on either side of a hard edge, and clamping to the local extremes removes them.

Algorithm

  1. Reference blur. For σ ≤ 4 a true Gaussian kernel, separable (half width 3σ). Above that, three chained box blurs: the variance of three boxes is 3·((2r+1)²−1)/12, so r = (√(4σ²+1) − 1)/2. Cost independent of radius — O(n) instead of O(n·σ) — and past σ = 4 the two agree to better than 1/255.
  2. Difference d = v − blur. If |d| ≤ threshold/255 the pixel is emitted unchanged.
  3. Sharpen v + amount·d.
  4. Halo suppression. Take the 3×3 min/max of the original, clamp step 3 into [lo, hi], then blend towards the unclamped value by fringe.

What step 4 does is worth stating plainly: on a perfect step edge it is a no-op (there is no transition left to steepen), and on a soft edge it steepens without overshoot. At 100% the result can never grow a fringe, at the cost of losing very small highlight overflow — use 30–50% if you want the PS-style "glowing highlights" kind of sharpness.

Recipes

  • Texture: radius=1.0 amount=120 threshold=3 fringe=100
  • Local contrast: radius=15 amount=40 threshold=0 fringe=60
  • Final output pass: radius=0.8 amount=90 threshold=2 fringe=100, after resizing

Testing
