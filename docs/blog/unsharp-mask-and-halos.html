锐化为什么会长白边，以及怎么让它不长

2026.11.18 约 2 分钟锐化USM光晕

USM 的本质是把差值加回去，代价是过冲。PS 用「淡化」和去卷积兜，我们换成一条更便宜的办法：把结果夹回邻域的极值。

USM（Unsharp Mask）的名字来自暗房：拿一张模糊过的负片当遮罩，原片减掉它，边缘就浮出来了。数字版是同一件事，写成一行：

$$\text{out} = v + a \cdot (v - \text{blur}(v))$$

a 是数量，blur 的半径决定「多宽的边缘才算边缘」。差值越大越锐，问题也就出在差值上。

过冲：白边是从哪来的

理想阶跃边：左边 0，右边 1。模糊之后，边缘两侧各出现一段过渡，比如左边紧邻的像素被抬到 0.15，右边紧邻的被压到 0.85。

于是：

位置原值 v模糊 blurv − blura=1 时的输出
暗侧紧邻0.000.15−0.15−0.15
亮侧紧邻1.000.85+0.151.15

差值加回去，暗侧被推到 0 以下、亮侧被推到 1 以上。8 位缓冲会把它们夹回 0 和 1，看起来「只是变硬了一点」；但只要这一层用浮点缓冲、或者叠了混合模式，超出的部分就会真的显示出来——暗侧一条黑边、亮侧一条白边。这就是 fringe（光晕）。

数量越大、半径越大，过冲越明显。所以「锐化过头」在画面上最典型的症状不是「太锐」，而是边缘发亮、发脏。

三条常见的补法

  1. 阈值。差值小于 threshold/255 的像素原样输出。它防的是噪点被放大， 对真正的强边一点用都没有——强边的差值恰恰最大。
  2. 淡化高光/阴影。PS 智能锐化里的那两个滑杆：亮部少锐一点、暗部少锐一点。 方向对，但它是全局的，会把真正需要锐化的中间调也一起收力。
  3. 去卷积。PS 智能锐化的「移去镜头模糊」是盲反卷积，从根上减少过冲， 代价是慢，而且对参数敏感。

我们的第四条：把结果夹回邻域极值

对每个像素，取原图 3×3 邻域的 [lo, hi]，把锐化结果夹进去，再按 fringe （0–100%）与未夹版本混合：

$$\text{out} = (1-f)\cdot\big(v + a\,d\big) + f\cdot \mathrm{clamp}\big(v + a\,d,\; lo,\; hi\big)$$

它为什么有效，看两个极端就清楚了：

  • 硬边：3×3 邻域里本来就有 0 也有 1，[lo, hi] = [0, 1]，夹取等于什么都没做—— 而硬边本来也没法更陡，所以这里是不锐化，正好。
  • 软边：过渡带的邻域极值就是过渡的两端，夹取把结果压在端点之间， 过渡被压陡，但绝不越过端点。

所以 fringe=100 时，这张图在数学上不可能多出白边或黑边。代价是高光的微弱溢出（比如镜面反光那一小撮过曝）会被一起压平——想要 PS 那种「亮部发光」的观感，把 fringe 调到 30–50，留一点过冲。

大半径怎么办

小半径（σ ≤ 4）用真高斯核，可分离卷积，代价与半径成正比。但「局部对比 / 清澈感」那种用法半径会到 15 甚至 50，O(n·σ) 在 4000 万像素上就慢了。

大半径退化成三次盒式模糊串联：三次盒式的方差是 3·((2r+1)²−1)/12，反解半径

$$r = \frac{\sqrt{4\sigma^2 + 1} - 1}{2}$$

代价与半径无关（O(n)，每像素固定几次加减），而 σ ≥ 4 时它与真高斯的分歧已经小于 1/255 —— 在 8 位输出上量不出来，16 位上也只是最后一两位。

参数怎么选

目的radiusamountthresholdfringe
纹理锐化1.0120%3100

缩图前最后一刀0.890%2100

顺序上，先降噪再锐化。USM 会把噪点当边缘放大，而 lb:reduce-noise 恰好只磨平坦区、不动边缘，两者是配套的。

自检

python tools/check_noise_sharpen.py 里跟锐化有关的几项：

  • amount=0 与 threshold=255 时逐位恒等；
  • 开 / 关光晕抑制，对比硬边两侧的过冲：fringe=100 时过冲必须为 0；
  • 软边过渡的级数变少（确实变陡了），且结果不越过两端；
  • 硬边在完全抑制时与输入相同；
  • alpha 原样透传。

菜单在 SuperMrWang → Sharpen → Unsharp Mask。

Why sharpening grows white fringes, and how to stop it

2026.11.18 1 min readSharpenUSMHalos

Unsharp masking is just adding the difference back, and the bill is overshoot. Photoshop pays it with fading and deconvolution; we pay it with something cheaper — clamping the result to the neighbourhood extremes.

Unsharp masking comes from the darkroom: a blurred negative serves as a mask, the original is subtracted from it, and edges float up. The digital version is the same idea in one line:

$$\text{out} = v + a \cdot (v - \text{blur}(v))$$

a is the amount; the blur radius decides what counts as an edge. The larger the difference, the sharper — and that difference is exactly where the trouble lives.

Overshoot: where the fringe comes from

Take an ideal step edge: 0 on the left, 1 on the right. After blurring, both sides have a transition; say the pixel just left of the edge is lifted to 0.15 and the one just right of it is pulled down to 0.85.

Positionvblurv − bluroutput at a=1
dark side0.000.15−0.15−0.15
bright side1.000.85+0.151.15

Adding the difference back pushes the dark side below zero and the bright side above one. An 8-bit buffer clamps both, so it merely looks harder. But on a float buffer, or through a blend mode, the excess is real: a dark line on one side, a bright line on the other. That is the fringe.

The larger the amount and the radius, the bigger the overshoot. So the classic symptom of over-sharpening is not "too sharp" — it is edges that glow and look dirty.

Three usual remedies

  1. Threshold. Pixels whose difference is below threshold/255 pass through untouched. This stops noise being amplified, and does nothing at all for a genuine hard edge — where the difference is largest.
  2. Fade highlights / shadows. The two sliders in Photoshop's Smart Sharpen. Right direction, but global: it also lets go of the midtones that most need sharpening.
  3. Deconvolution. "Remove lens blur" is blind deconvolution. It attacks overshoot at the root, at the cost of speed and sensitivity to its parameters.

Our fourth: clamp to the neighbourhood extremes

For each pixel, take [lo, hi] over the original 3×3 neighbourhood, clamp the sharpened value into it, then blend with the unclamped version by fringe (0–100%):

$$\text{out} = (1-f)\cdot\big(v + a\,d\big) + f\cdot \mathrm{clamp}\big(v + a\,d,\; lo,\; hi\big)$$

Two extremes explain why it works:

  • Hard edge: the 3×3 neighbourhood already contains both 0 and 1, so [lo, hi] = [0, 1] and the clamp does nothing — and a hard edge cannot get any steeper anyway, so "nothing" is exactly right.
  • Soft edge: the extremes are the two ends of the transition, so the clamped result is pressed towards the ends, never past them. The ramp gets steeper; it never overshoots.

With fringe=100 the result cannot gain a bright or dark fringe. The cost is that genuine highlight spill — that small blown specular patch — is flattened too. For the Photoshop "glowing highlights" look, set fringe to 30–50 and keep a little overshoot.

Large radii

Small radii (σ ≤ 4) use a true Gaussian, separable, cost proportional to the radius. But the local-contrast / clarity style wants radius 15, sometimes 50, and O(n·σ) on forty megapixels is slow.

Large radii fall back to three chained box blurs. Three boxes have variance 3·((2r+1)²−1)/12, so the radius is

$$r = \frac{\sqrt{4\sigma^2 + 1} - 1}{2}$$

The cost is independent of the radius (O(n), a fixed handful of adds per pixel), and at σ ≥ 4 the difference from a true Gaussian is below 1/255 — unmeasurable on 8-bit output, and the last bit or two at 16-bit.

Choosing parameters

Goalradiusamountthresholdfringe
Texture sharpening1.0120%3100

Order matters: denoise first, sharpen second. USM treats noise as edges; lb:reduce-noise smooths only flat areas and leaves edges alone, so the two belong together.

Self-check

The sharpening assertions in python tools/check_noise_sharpen.py:

  • amount=0 and threshold=255 are bit-exact identities;
  • with halo suppression on and off, compare overshoot on both sides of a hard edge: at fringe=100 it must be zero;
  • a soft edge ends up with fewer steps (it really did get steeper) without crossing its ends;
  • a hard edge is unchanged under full suppression;
  • alpha passes through untouched.

The menu entry is SuperMrWang → Sharpen → Unsharp Mask.
